Why we ask. Your class teacher confirms your registration, and organizers need to know that a student taking part in an event was near the campus. We therefore compare one location reading and, if you choose to attach one, one photo against reference pictures of the campus.
Your location is only read when you press the button below, is used for this event only, and is deleted automatically after {{ retention_days }} days. It is never tracked in the background. Camera access is optional and the photo is sent only when you run the comparison.
A location match and a similar photo are indicative signals, not proof that you were on campus. Every submission is reviewed by a person, and an uncertain result is never treated as misconduct. If GPS is inaccurate or location permission is unavailable, that is recorded as “inconclusive” and a reviewer decides.
