How to Use CampusPulse

Back to Home
This manual walks you through the full flow for students, organizers, HoD reviewers, and administrators using the event management system.
01

Open the home page and create an account

On the welcome page, you can explore the platform and choose the action you need.

  • Click Login if you already have an account.
  • Click Create Account to register as a student or organizer.
  • Use the admin portal only for the predefined admin dashboard.

02

Create your student or organizer profile

Complete the registration form with your full name, email address, and a valid password.

  • Only Student and Organizer roles are available during signup.
  • Email addresses must be unique. Duplicate emails will be rejected.
  • Use a password with at least 8 characters for stronger protection.

03

Log in to your account

Use the standard login page for student, organizer, and provisioned HoD accounts.

  • Enter your registered email and password.
  • Use the Show toggle to view your password.
  • If you forgot your password, use the Forgot Password? link.

04

Student workflow

Students can explore approved, upcoming events and register before the deadline.

  • Open the student dashboard to see available events.
  • Register only for approved events with available seats.
  • Cancel your registration before the registration deadline.
  • After an event is marked completed, registered students can submit one rating from 1 to 5 and optional comments.

05

Organizer workflow

Organizers create events and manage the lifecycle from submission to completion.

  • Create a new event with title, venue, date, and participant limit.
  • Submit the event for HoD verification; after the HoD forwards it, the administrator gives final approval.
  • Use the Organizer profile card to select whether you are a class incharge.
  • Once the event date has passed, mark the approved event as completed to unlock feedback collection.

06

HoD verification workflow

The Head of the Department verifies organizer-submitted programs before they reach the administrator.

  • Open the HoD Dashboard and review the title, description, venue, schedule, capacity, fee, and organizer details.
  • Choose Verify & send to admin when the program details are correct.
  • Choose Reject when the program needs correction and should not proceed.

07

Admin workflow

Administrators provide final approval for HoD-verified programs, calculate PA points, and export reports.

  • Use the separate admin login page at /admin/login.
  • Approve or reject programs that the HoD has verified and forwarded.
  • Calculate PA points after events complete and generate PDF or Excel reports.

08

Export reports and review results

Once events are completed, the admin can export records for academic evaluation.

  • Generate a PDF summary report with the academic year and event data.
  • Export an Excel workbook with the full scoring and totals.
  • Use these reports to track productivity, event outcomes, and PA point allocation.

Quick tips

Use unique emails

Duplicate emails are not allowed so each account stays secure and traceable.

Protect your password

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and the app uses protected forms and session handling.

Follow deadlines

Students must register before the deadline and organizers should complete events on time.
